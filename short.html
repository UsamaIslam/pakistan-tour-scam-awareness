Pakistan Northern Area Tour Scam Summary: Exposing Tour Edge & Tours Avenue

Hunza Tour & Naran Kaghan Tour Safety Alert

Planning a Northern Area tour, Hunza tour, or Naran Kaghan tour? To help you avoid widespread tour scams in Pakistan, here is a verified summary of the hotel fraud, driver fatigue safety issues, and contract violations from our trip:

  • Unnotified Sub-Contracting: Booked with Tour Edge (Lahore), but they secretly outsourced the entire service to Tours Avenue.
  • Late Departure & Gaslighting: Left Lahore 2 hours late. Changed the pickup spot last minute and dropped fake pins to blame us for delays.
  • Hotel Theft: Skipped our confirmed night at Jovial Gold Hotel Naran without any refund, apology, or alternative compensation.
  • Extreme Safety Negligence: Forced the driver to stay awake and drive a coaster for 24 hours straight, causing him to fall asleep at the wheel on mountain roads.
  • Graveyard Downgrade: Attempted to force us into a sketchy hotel (Hunza Elite) located right next to a cemetery.
  • Extortion Attempt: Unilaterally changed the return stop and demanded an extra 1,000 PKR to access a third-class campsite at Babusar Top. We refused to pay.
