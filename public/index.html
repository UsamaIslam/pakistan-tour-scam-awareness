Avoid Tour Scams when Booking a Tour to Hunza

This public awareness portal is built to warn travelers planning a tour to Hunza or a tour to northern areas of Pakistan about widespread tour scams. Specifically, we document the deceptive strategies, hotel booking fraud, and safety risks of Tour Edge (Lahore, also searched as touredge) and its operational front Tours Avenue Pvt Ltd (also searched as toursavenue).

Major Red Flags Documented:

  • Unnotified Sub-Contracting: Secretly offloaded our booking to Tours Avenue, a lower-tier operator, without our knowledge.
  • Hotel Theft (Jovial Gold Hotel Naran): Skipped our pre-paid luxury stay at Naran entirely, pocketing our funds with zero refund or apology.
  • Endangering Lives: Pushed an experienced driver to operate a coaster non-stop for 24 hours, causing him to fall asleep at the wheel on mountain roads.
  • Attempted Extortion: Unilaterally altered the itinerary and demanded extra money on the road, which we firmly rejected.
  • Graveyard Hotel Downgrade: Attempted to house us in "Hunza Elite," a property located in an abandoned area right next to a cemetery.

Traveler's Pre-Booking Verification Toolkit

Before transferring any booking fees or token money, use these 5 steps to verify if the operator is selling you a fake package:

1
Call Hotels Directly (e.g. Jovial Gold Hotel Naran)

Do not rely on screenshots or PDF confirmations sent by agents. Contact the hotel directly, ask if a booking has been confirmed and fully paid under your name, and verify the dates.

2
Request Driver Profile & Vehicle Registration

Demand the vehicle license plate and the names/CNICs of both the primary and backup drivers. Legitimate companies will supply this. Warn them that you do not accept single-driver 24-hour schedules.

3
Get a Written "No-Subcontracting" Clause

Ensure you have a written statement on WhatsApp or official email stating that your tour will be executed solely by the booking agency, not outsourced to third-class entities like Tours Avenue.

4
Google Maps Search Promised Hotels

Lookup every hotel in your itinerary (e.g. Hunza Elite) on satellite maps. Check recent visitor photos and search reviews for mentions of cemetery borders, construction status, or remote isolated areas.

5
Compare with Self-Booking Expenses

Book a Daewoo or Faisal Movers executive ticket, and call luxury hotels in Naran and Hunza directly. Self-planning is 30% to 40% cheaper, safer, and allows you to walk away from bad services.
